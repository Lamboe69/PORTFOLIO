About Me

Lamboe is a self-taught mobile and AI developer and Year II Software Engineering student at Makerere University (SCIT), Kampala. He co-founded two active startups — TutorUG, an AI-powered localized learning platform, and CampusKart/UniMart, a campus marketplace app — and currently leads technical development on KawaAI, an AI-powered coffee farming assistant for Ugandan farmers.

He taught himself Kotlin and Jetpack Compose from scratch and is currently deepening his backend skills with Spring Boot (Kotlin). Beyond app development, he builds algorithmic trading systems (Pine Script, MQL5, Python) and has funded prop trading accounts.

His long-term vision is to bring technology-driven development back to Northern Uganda, including building infrastructure and mentoring the next generation of engineers there, the way his own mentor Walter shaped his path.

KawaAI — AI Coffee Farming Assistant

Technical Lead

An Android app that helps Ugandan coffee farmers with AI-powered crop disease diagnosis and planting guidance. Features a Planting Advisor that factors in rainfall onset, post-planting rainfall duration, and soil temperature; a disease diagnosis model (EfficientNet-B0, fine-tuned on the RoCoLe and JMuBEN datasets, running via TFLite for offline use); a farm calendar; and offline mode for low-connectivity rural use. All data is sourced from real providers: Open-Meteo, CHIRPS, and NOAA.
